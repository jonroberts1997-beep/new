2Pick Your Location

Sunniest spot with good drainage, nearest your kitchen door.
What matters most
  • Sun: 6+ hours of direct sun. Check at 9am, noon & 4pm.
  • Water: close to a spigot; drains well, no pooling after rain.
  • Access: near the kitchen door; easy to bring in materials.
  • Soil: the healthiest ground you have (tests below).
Quick soil tests
  • Compaction: push rebar in — stops at in.
  • Drainage: fill a 12" hole — empties in hrs (1–4 ideal)
  • Worms per shovelful (5+ = healthy) · then look, feel, smell

5Crops & Calendar

Start with your Foundation & Meal Maker staples. Count back from frost dates.
Top cropsCool / WarmStart indoorsTransplant / Sow
Cool season (around frost): kale, collards, lettuce, peas, carrots, beets, onions, garlic · Warm season (after last frost): tomatoes, beans, squash, sweet potatoes, okra, corn
Succession = no empty beds: garlic → fall carrots · spring peas → summer beans · lettuce → tomatoes. Re-sow lettuce monthly, beans every 2–3 wks.

Winter to-do

Pick ONE to do this week.
  • Order seeds (buy here today!)
  • Source compost & mulch
  • Rake fall leaves for mulch
  • Flag the spot, take sun photos
  • Draw the basemap
  • Sheet-mulch your first beds
Healthy-garden habits
  • Walk it 3× a week
  • Mix in flowers & herbs for beneficial insects
  • Pests: handpick → barrier → soap → organic spray
4 Principles of Living Soil
  1. Minimize disturbance — build beds once, never till
  2. Keep it covered — leaves, straw, clippings, chips
  3. Keep roots in the ground — no bare beds
  4. Add life — compost, worm castings, diversity
